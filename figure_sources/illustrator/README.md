# Illustrator figure sources

`figure4_predictive_processing_initial.ai` is the PDF-compatible Illustrator source supplied from `AllenNeuralDynamics/openscope_perspective`. `figure-01-predictive-processing.png` is the 3200×2400 publication rendering used for Figure 1. Source URL, repository revision, checksums, dimensions, and conversion method are recorded in `figure-01-predictive-processing.provenance.json`.

`neuropixel_logo.ai`, `mesoscope_logo.ai`, and `slap2_logo.ai` are the editable platform marks used as modality cues in generated figures. Transparent PNG24 derivatives live under `figure_sources/media/platform-logos/`; source and rendered checksums, dimensions, Illustrator export settings, and post-export framing are recorded in `platform-logos.provenance.json`.

`figure-01-panel-c-training-cohorts.ai` and `figure-01-panel-c-modality-cohorts.ai` preserve the editable design language used for Figure 1C's cross-modality cohort diagram. `figure-02-stimulus-timeline.ai` and `figure-02-detailed-blocks.ai` provide the two editable panels used in Figure 2's static control-architecture view. Their source and rendered checksums are recorded in `experimental-design-sources.provenance.json`.

Generated figure text uses `Myriad Pro, Arial, sans-serif` to match the Adobe panels, with font sizes normalized to a 1200-unit reference canvas. Platform names use a shared 20-unit reference tier, and nearby labels or artwork use an 8-unit local spacing margin. Myriad Pro is supplied by Adobe Illustrator and is not vendored; Arial is the publication fallback. `IBM Plex Mono` remains reserved for aligned technical values and identifiers.
