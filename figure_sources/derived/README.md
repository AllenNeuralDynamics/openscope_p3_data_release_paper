# Derived figure assets

These PNGs are deterministic crops of Google Doc-rendered source images. The crop boxes, original and output dimensions, purpose, and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `cropped-figures.provenance.json`. The importer verifies both source and derived checksums before copying them into `images/figures/imported/`.

- The former Figure 2 A-C crop is retained as a source asset; its workflow panel is embedded in the generated two-panel Figure 1.
- The former panel D crop is retained for historical provenance. Figure 3 now uses the editable timeline and detailed-block Illustrator sources documented under `figure_sources/illustrator/`.
- Figure 4 removes the Neuronal traces column and retains cohorts, rigs, mouse platforms, and brain-targeting schematics.
