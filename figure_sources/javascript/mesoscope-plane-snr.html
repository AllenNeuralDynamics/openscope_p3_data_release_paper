Selecting a definition switches every panel. The plane strip orders the eight simultaneously acquired planes by depth within area; the map fills each segmented ROI with its value on a shared per-metric scale; the scatter plots every ROI in the selected plane against its segmented area in pixels; and selecting an ROI draws its 60-second dF/F excerpt with detected events. Each definition is computed over the complete recording, not over the displayed excerpt.
