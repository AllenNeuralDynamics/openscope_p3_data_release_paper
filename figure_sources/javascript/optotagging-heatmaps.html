Interactive figure legend

Laser-aligned responses to raised-cosine, 5 Hz, and 40 Hz stimulation are shown for non-noise units from three public Neuropixels sessions near the 50th, 80th, and 95th percentiles of optotagged-cell yield, using 1-ms time bins. Rows are baseline z-scored per unit. Anatomical filtering precedes ordering by firing rate during each condition's exact laser-on intervals; strongest responses appear at the top. All panels share the displayed symmetric color scale.
